Welcome to Huyen Tuyen Rice

Huyen Tuyen Rice is a trusted provider of fresh, high-quality rice, nutritious nuts, seeds, and whole grains for families. We carefully select our products from the finest farms to ensure clean ingredients, natural flavors, and the best nutrition for your daily meals.

Our mission is to bring wholesome, safe, and delicious food to every Vietnamese family. From premium ST25 rice to healthy brown rice, from crunchy almonds to nutritious chia seeds � we offer a wide range of products that support a healthy lifestyle.

We believe that good food starts with good ingredients. That is why we work directly with farmers and trusted suppliers to bring you the freshest products at fair prices. Every grain of rice, every nut, and every seed we sell is carefully inspected and packed with care.

Thank you for choosing Huyen Tuyen Rice. We look forward to serving you and your family with the best quality products.
