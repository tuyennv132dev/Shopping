Warm Oatmeal with Nuts - Comforting Breakfast

By Admin / Comments (7) / 19/05/2026

There's nothing quite like a warm bowl of oatmeal on a cool morning. Packed with Huyen Tuyen's premium rolled oats and topped with a variety of nutritious nuts, this breakfast is not only comforting but also incredibly healthy. Rich in fiber, protein, and healthy fats, it will keep you full and energized until lunchtime.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of Huyen Tuyen rolled oats
  • 2 cups of milk or water
  • 1/4 cup mixed nuts (almonds, walnuts, cashews)
  • 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Fresh fruits (banana slices, berries) for topping
  • A pinch of salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Cook the Oatmeal - In a saucepan, bring 2 cups of milk or water to a gentle boil. Add Huyen Tuyen rolled oats and a pinch of salt. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the oats are tender and creamy.

Step 2: Add Sweetness and Flavor - Stir in honey or brown sugar and cinnamon. Mix well to combine. Adjust sweetness to your preference.

Step 3: Prepare the Nuts - While the oatmeal cooks, lightly toast the mixed nuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until fragrant. Chop them roughly for easier eating.

Step 4: Assemble and Serve - Pour the warm oatmeal into serving bowls. Top with toasted nuts, fresh banana slices, and berries. Drizzle with extra honey if desired. Serve immediately while warm.

This Warm Oatmeal with Nuts is the perfect breakfast for cold mornings, post-workout recovery, or whenever you need a nourishing meal. Customize with your favorite nuts and fruits for endless variations!
