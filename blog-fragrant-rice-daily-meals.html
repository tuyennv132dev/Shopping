Fragrant Rice for Daily Meals - Tips & Recipes

By Admin / Comments (8) / 18/05/2026

Fragrant rice is a beloved staple in Vietnamese households, known for its delicate aroma and fluffy texture. Whether you're cooking a simple family dinner or preparing a special meal, mastering the art of cooking fragrant rice can elevate your daily dining experience. In this guide, we'll share tips and recipes to help you make the most of Huyen Tuyen's premium fragrant rice.

Why Choose Fragrant Rice for Daily Meals?

  • Natural floral aroma that enhances any dish
  • Soft, fluffy texture when cooked perfectly
  • Versatile - pairs well with meats, vegetables, and soups
  • Rich in essential carbohydrates for daily energy

Tips for Cooking Perfect Fragrant Rice

Tip 1: Rinse Properly - Rinse 1 cup of Huyen Tuyen fragrant rice in cold water 2-3 times until the water runs clear. This removes excess starch and prevents the rice from becoming too sticky.

Tip 2: Measure Water Correctly - For fragrant rice, use a 1:1.2 ratio of rice to water. For example, 1 cup of rice requires 1.2 cups of water. This ratio creates perfectly fluffy grains.

Tip 3: Use the Right Cooking Method - In a rice cooker, simply set and forget. On the stovetop, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-18 minutes. Do not lift the lid during cooking.

Tip 4: Rest Before Serving - After cooking, let the rice rest for 5-10 minutes with the lid on. This allows the steam to finish cooking the grains and results in a fluffier texture. Fluff gently with a fork before serving.

Simple Everyday Recipe: Garlic Fragrant Rice

Step 1 - Rinse 1 cup of Huyen Tuyen fragrant rice and drain.

Step 2 - In a pot, sautÃ© 2 cloves of minced garlic in 1 tbsp of butter or oil until fragrant (about 1 minute).

Step 3 - Add the rinsed rice and stir for 1 minute to coat with garlic butter.

Step 4 - Add 1.2 cups of water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-18 minutes.

Step 5 - Remove from heat, let rest for 5 minutes, then fluff and serve. Enjoy this aromatic garlic rice with grilled meats, stir-fried vegetables, or your favorite Vietnamese dishes.

With these tips and recipes, you'll be able to enjoy restaurant-quality fragrant rice right at home. Huyen Tuyen's fragrant rice is carefully selected from the best paddies, ensuring consistent quality and authentic aroma in every grain.
